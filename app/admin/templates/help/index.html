Getting Started

Welcome to the JOURN3Y admin dashboard. Here's what you can do:

  • Dashboard — See an overview of your team: total users, sites, voice notes, and timesheet entries.
  • Team — Add and manage your team members. Assign skills (voice notes, timesheets, site updates) to control what each person can do when they call Jill.
  • Sites — Create and manage your construction sites. Generate QR codes for on-site sign-on tracking.
  • Settings — Configure company-wide features like QR Sign-On, SMS reminders, and phone numbers.
  • Reports — View QR Sign-On activity, timesheet entries, and voice notes. Filter by date, user, or site and export to CSV.

First steps for a new company

  1. Go to Team and add your workers. Make sure to include their phone number with country code (e.g. +61 for Australia).
  2. Assign skills to each team member (e.g. "timesheet" so they can log hours by calling Jill).
  3. Go to Sites and add your construction sites.
  4. If using QR Sign-Ons, go to Settings and enable QR Sign-On, then generate QR codes from the Sites page.

Team Management

Adding a new team member

  1. Go to Team from the sidebar.
  2. Click the + Add User button (top-right).
  3. Fill in their name, phone number (with country code, e.g. +61412345678), email (optional), and role.
  4. Click Create.

The phone number is how Jill identifies your team members when they call in. It must match the number they'll call from.

Editing a team member

Click the pencil icon next to any user to update their name, phone, email, or role.

Activating / Deactivating users

Use the toggle switch in the Active column. When deactivated (red), the user cannot use Jill's voice features. Their data is preserved.

Managing skills

Skills control what a user can do when they call Jill:

  • Timesheet — Log hours, start/end times, and work descriptions by phone.
  • Voice Notes — Record voice messages that are transcribed and stored.
  • Site Updates — Report construction site progress, issues, or safety concerns.

To add a skill: click the + dropdown next to their skill badges and select a skill.

To remove a skill: hover over the skill badge and click the × button.

SMS reminders

If SMS reminders are enabled in Settings, you can enrol individual users using the SMS checkbox in the table. Enrolled users will receive reminder texts to sign off at the end of their day.

Sites

Creating a site

  1. Go to Sites from the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add Site.
  3. Enter the site name (required) and address (optional).
  4. Click Create.

When your team calls Jill to log timesheets, she will ask which site they worked at. The site names you create here are what Jill uses to match their responses.

Editing & deleting sites

Click the pencil icon to edit a site's name or address. Click the trash icon to delete it.

Note: Sites marked with the "Overheads" badge are special. They represent non-site-specific work (admin, office duties, general work). These cannot be deleted.

QR codes for sites

If QR Sign-On is enabled in Settings, you can generate a QR code for each site:

  1. Click Generate QR next to the site.
  2. Once generated, you can download the QR code as a PDF to print and display on-site.
  3. Workers scan the QR code with their phone to sign on when they arrive at site.

Settings

Company information

Your company name and timezone are shown at the top. These are configured by your JOURN3Y administrator.

QR Sign-On settings

Click QR Sign-On Settings to expand the panel:

  • Enable QR Sign-On — Master toggle. Turn this on to use QR code site tracking. Once enabled, you can generate QR codes from the Sites page.
  • SMS Reminders — When enabled, enrolled team members receive text reminders to sign off at the end of their day.
  • Reminder times — Set when the first and second reminder texts are sent (e.g. 5:30 PM and 7:00 PM).
  • Post Sign-On Message — A customisable message shown to workers after they scan a QR code (e.g. "Remember to complete your health and safety sign-in form").

Phone numbers

Click Phone Numbers to expand:

  • Jill Phone Number — The number your workers call to speak to Jill. This number is included in SMS reminder messages.
  • Manager Phone Number — Shown to unregistered users who scan a QR code but aren't in the system yet.

QR Sign-Ons Report

How QR Sign-Ons work

  1. A worker arrives at site and scans the QR code with their phone.
  2. They're signed on automatically. The system records the time and site.
  3. At the end of the day, the worker calls Jill to log their timesheet. Jill asks for start time, end time, and what they did.
  4. Once the timesheet is submitted, the sign-on status changes from "Awaiting Jill" to "Complete".

Viewing sign-on data

Go to Reports → QR Sign-Ons. You'll see:

  • Stats cards at the top: Total Sign-Ons, Awaiting Jill (not yet called), and Complete.
  • Table showing each sign-on with user, site, scan time, start/end times, hours, and status.

Filtering

Use the filter bar to narrow results by:

  • Date range — Pick a from/to date, or use quick presets: Today, This Week, This Month.
  • User — Filter to a specific team member.
  • Status — All, Active (still on-site), or Signed Off (complete).

Editing start/end times

If a worker's times need correcting, click the pencil icon next to the Start Time or End Time. Enter the correct time and click Save. Hours are recalculated automatically.

Viewing call transcripts

If the worker called Jill, a speech bubble icon appears in the row. Click the row to expand and read the full conversation transcript.

CSV export

Click the Export CSV button to download the filtered data as a spreadsheet.

Timesheets Report

Three views

The Timesheets report has three tabs at the top:

  • All Users Summary — A high-level table showing total hours, days worked, and average hours per user. Click View Details to drill into a specific user.
  • Individual User — Detailed list of every timesheet entry for a selected user. Shows date, site, start/end times, hours, and work description.
  • By Site — Groups entries by construction site. Shows site cards with total hours, worker count, and recent entries. Click a card to see all entries for that site.

Filtering

Use the filters at the top to narrow results:

  • Date range with quick presets (Today, This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month).
  • User dropdown — filter to one team member.
  • Site dropdown — filter to one site (in By Site view).

Editing times

In the Individual User or By Site detail views, click the pencil icon next to a start or end time to correct it. Hours are recalculated automatically when you save.

Viewing call transcripts

Click any row with a speech bubble icon to expand and see the full conversation between the worker and Jill.

CSV export

Click Export CSV to download the currently filtered data. The export matches whatever view and filters you have active.

How Jill Works

What is Jill?

Jill is your AI voice assistant. Your team members call Jill's phone number to log timesheets, record voice notes, or report site updates — all by simply talking on the phone.

How a call works

  1. A team member calls the Jill phone number (shown in Settings).
  2. Jill identifies them by their phone number (must match what's in the Team page).
  3. Based on the skills assigned to them, Jill asks what they'd like to do.
  4. For timesheets: Jill asks which site, start/end times, what work was done, and plans for tomorrow.
  5. For voice notes: Jill records their message and saves the transcript.
  6. For site updates: Jill asks about progress, issues, or safety concerns at a specific site.
  7. Everything is saved automatically and appears in your reports.

What if a call doesn't work?

  • Check that the user's phone number in the Team page matches the number they're calling from (including country code).
  • Make sure the user is active (green toggle in Team).
  • Make sure they have the relevant skill assigned (e.g. "timesheet").
  • If they're getting an overhead/admin site, they may be using keywords like "office" or "admin" — Jill automatically routes these to the Overheads site.

Your Account

Changing your password

Click your avatar (the circle with your initial) in the top-right corner, then select Change Password.

You'll need to enter your current password and choose a new one. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include an uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character.

First login

When your administrator creates your account, you'll be given a temporary password. On your first login, you'll be asked to change it before you can access the dashboard.

Logging out

Click your avatar, then Logout. Your session will be cleared.

Troubleshooting

Jill doesn't recognise a team member

  • Make sure their phone number includes the country code (e.g. +61 for Australia). The number must match exactly.
  • Check they are active on the Team page (green toggle).

A worker can't log timesheets / voice notes

  • Go to Team and check the user has the relevant skill assigned.
  • If the skill doesn't appear in the dropdown, it may be disabled for your company. Contact your JOURN3Y administrator.

QR code won't scan

  • Make sure QR Sign-On is enabled in Settings.
  • Ensure the QR code has been generated for that site (check the Sites page for a green QR status).
  • The phone's camera needs to be able to read the QR clearly — check the print isn't damaged or too small.

Timesheet data looks wrong

  • You can edit start/end times directly from the QR Sign-Ons or Timesheets report. Click the pencil icon next to the time.
  • Changes made on the QR Sign-Ons page and the Timesheets page both update the same underlying record — they're linked.

Something else not working?

Contact your JOURN3Y administrator for further assistance.
